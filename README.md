# BAKE Studio

A website design studio prototype.

1. **Brand**: enter your business details, then pick a logo, hero image and illustrations.
2. **Templates**: browse 36 homepage layouts that already use your brand. Filter by style or by the sections you need, favourite the ones you like, preview them on phone, tablet and desktop, and pick one.

Your choices are saved in your browser.

Live site: https://deltaowleyes.github.io/bake-studio/
